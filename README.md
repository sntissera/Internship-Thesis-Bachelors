# Characterization of spatial heterogeneity in glioblastoma using transcriptional entropy.

## Overview

Glioblastoma (GBM) is a highly aggressive brain tumor known for strong molecular and transcriptional heterogeneity. This study investigated transcriptional heterogeneity of glioblastoma using transcriptional entropy to quantify heterogeneity across spatial spots. Spatial Transcriptomic data on glioblastoma were used for the study. Spatial Transcriptomics provides a way to investigate this heterogeneity while retaining the spatial organization of the tissue. 

The study used six spatial transcriptomics samples generated using 10X Genomics Visium platform, which was previous generated by Ren et al. and publicly available on Gene Expression Omnibus. I developed an entropy based bioinformatics pipeline to quantify transcriptional heterogeneity across spatial spots and identify genes and biological programs associated with heterogeneous tumor regions. The pipeline was also used to characterize discrete and continuous entropy based spatial regions on the tissue to identify stemness driving regions. 

This study integrates spatial transcriptomic, entropy based analysis, statistical modelling and functional enrichment analysis to characterize glioblastoma heterogeneity.
