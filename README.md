# Characterization of spatial heterogeneity in glioblastoma using transcriptional entropy.

## Overview

Glioblastoma (GBM) is a highly aggressive brain tumor known for strong molecular and transcriptional heterogeneity. This study investigated transcriptional heterogeneity in GBM using transcriptional entropy to quantify variability in gene expression across spatially resolved tissue spots. 

The study analyzed six glioblastoma spatial transcriptomics samples generated using the 10X Genomics Visium platform, previously generated by Ren et al. and publicly available through the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO). Spatial transcriptomics enables transcriptional heterogeneity to be investigated while preserving the spatial organization of the tumor tissue. 

I developed an entropy based bioinformatics pipeline to quantify transcriptional heterogeneity across spatial spots and identify genes and biological programs associated with heterogeneous tumor regions. The pipeline was further used to characterize discrete and continuous entropy based spatial regions, with the aim of identifying stemness related transcriptional programs. 

This study integrates spatial transcriptomic, entropy based analysis, statistical modelling and functional enrichment analysis to characterize transcriptional heterogeneity in glioblastoma.

## Purpose of the study

This study aims to investigate how transcriptional entropy reflects spatial heterogeneity in GBM within different biological contexts. It aims to determine whether regions with elevated entropy consistently correspond to stemness related transcriptional programs or whether they relect distinct biological processes depending on tissue context.

## Dataset

Original Dataset: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE194329

The dataset was composed of 6 glioblastoma samples, 5 tumor samples and 1 peritumor sample. The peritumor sample was used as a reference to compare with tumor associated entropy profiles. Raw sequences were already quality controlled by the authors. Raw sequencing and large processed datasets are not stored in this repository. The corresponding data source and publication is documented in the thesis.

## Pipeline

Spatial Transcriptomics Data -> Normalization using `SCTransform()` -> Transcriptional Entropy Calculation and Scaling -> Entropy Distribution -> Classification into discrete spatial regions using Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) -> Shared vs Sample specific gene extraction -> Functional Enrichment analysis -> Module score validation -> Correlation analysis -> Fisher's Exact test -> Visualization of both discrete and continuous entropy based spatial regions.

## Calculations

### Transcriptional Entropy

For each gene, transcriptional entropy was calculated across each spatial spot to quantify the variability of gene expression patterns. The resulting values were used to characterize spatial heterogeneity by means of Mean Entropy, standard deviation of entropy and entropy distributions.
